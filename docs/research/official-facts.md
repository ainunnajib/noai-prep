**IOAI-level eligibility** (IOAI Regulations §8.3.1, https://ioai-official.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/IOAI-regulations.pdf) [H]:
- Contestants must be **20 or younger on 1 July** of the IOAI year.
- They must have been **enrolled full-time in primary or secondary education on or after 1 December** of the previous year (home-schooled: not yet holding a high-school diploma).
- They must be selected through a recognised national competition.
- **Each accredited country is entitled to one team of up to four contestants.** The Board decides each year whether second teams are allowed. For IOAI 2027 the Sep 2026 meeting says one team per country.

6. Contact moved to noai@aisingapore.org.
7. Study guidance changed from "review the IOAI 2026 syllabus" to "review the IOAI syllabus".

**Official study guidance** [H, S2]:
- *"Students should review the **IOAI syllabus** and read extensively about each of the covered topics. As a starting point, a curated list of learning resources can be found on the NOAI page."*
- *"No prior AI experience is required for the Preliminary Round. However … students should be passionate and motivated to work through the available learning resources on their own."*
- *"The assessed curriculum is publicly available through free resources, and all students are expected to prepare independently."*

**IOAI syllabus.** The latest published version is the **IOAI 2026 Syllabus**, uploaded Oct 2025 (S17). No IOAI 2027 syllabus had been published as of 14 Oct 2026; the 2026 one appeared in Oct 2025, so the 2027 one may appear soon [M]. It sorts topics into Theory, Practice or Both:
1. **Foundational Skills & Classical ML.** Python, NumPy/Pandas, Matplotlib/Seaborn, scikit-learn, PyTorch basics, tensors, CPU/GPU. Linear and logistic regression, L1/L2, k-NN, decision trees, ensembles, SVM. K-means, PCA, t-SNE/UMAP, DBSCAN, hierarchical and spectral clustering. Metrics, under/overfitting, hyperparameter tuning, cross-validation, confusion matrix/ROC, feature engineering, data processing.
2. **Neural Networks & DL.** Perceptron, gradient descent, backprop, activations, losses, MLP, embeddings, pooling, attention, Transformers, autoencoders, SGD/mini-batch, Adam/AdamW, learning rates, dropout, early stopping, weight decay, initialisation, BatchNorm, fine-tuning (full and PEFT).
3. **Computer Vision.** Convolutions, classification, detection (YOLO/SSD/DETR), U-Net segmentation, ResNet, augmentation, GANs, self-supervised learning, CLIP, diffusion.
4. **NLP & Audio.** Text classification, BERT, language modelling, encoder-decoder, pre-trained LMs, HuBERT, Whisper, Qwen-Audio, Voxtral.

The syllabus states that "Topics not listed are considered excluded" and that "Breadth should be prioritized over depth".

Note that the NOAI **Mathematics** domain (linear algebra, calculus, probability, optimisation) and **Problem Solving / Debugging** are **NOAI-specific emphases**. They are not separate IOAI syllabus sections.

## 7. Contradictions and uncertainties (how I resolved each)

1. **Prelim size: 300 MCQs / 3 h vs 150 MCQs / 1.5 h.**
   - These are two different cycles, not a conflict. 300 in 3 h is NOAI 2026 (S3, S4, past paper). 150 in 1.5 h is NOAI 2027 (S1, S2 updated 14 Oct 2026).
   - The AISG main page (S4) is frozen at 11 Feb 2026 and must not be used for 2027.
2. **Interest window start: 1 Aug vs 3 Aug 2026.**
   - The 2027 page text and FAQ say 1 Aug 2026. The timeline graphic says "3 Aug 2026 to 15 Jan 2027".
   - Version history: 14 Jul "3 Aug 2026 onwards", then 14 Oct "Aug 2026 to 15 Jan 2027", then 14 Oct (current) "3 Aug 2026 to 15 Jan 2027".
   - The difference is immaterial now. **The end date, 15 Jan 2027, is consistent.**
3. **"Five domains" vs four listed areas.**
   - The FAQ (2026 and 2027) and the 2026 paper count five domains, with **Generative AI** separate. The 2027 page lists four areas, with GenAI inside AI/ML/DL.
   - Treat GenAI as a full domain (~1/5 of the 2026 prelim, ~59–60 Qs). [M]
   - The 2027 per-domain split is **not published**. ~30 Qs per domain is only my assumption from the 2026 split. [L]
4. **Number of NTU invitees: "Top 50–60" vs "Top 60" vs "top performers".**
   - The 2026 timeline says 50–60. The 2026 FAQ Q5 and the 2027 timeline say top 60. The 2027 FAQ says only "top performers".
   - I use **top 60** for 2027 [H], but the exact number is at NTU's discretion.
5. **IOAI 2026 location: Abu Dhabi vs Astana.**
   - The S4 FAQ Q17 (11 Feb 2026) says "IOAI 2026 in Abu Dhabi".
   - IOAI relocated it to **Astana, 2–8 Aug 2026** on **21 Mar 2026**, citing Middle East developments; the original host was MBZUAI.
   - **Astana is correct.**
6. **IOAI 2026 field size: 440 vs 466, and 95 vs 108 countries.**
   - 440 / 95 (MOE, AISG) counts Individual-Contest contestants. 466 / 108 (IOAI press release) adds 26 GAITE contestants and delegations.
   - The official results table has 440 ranked rows plus 26 in GAITE. Both figures are correct for their scope.
7. **Size of Singapore's 2027 team.**
   - Singapore has always sent **2 teams (8 students)**.
   - IOAI's Sep 2026 meeting says "**Starting from 2027, each country will participate with one national team**". IOAI Regulations cap a team at **4 contestants**, with a second team allowed only at Board discretion.
   - **No source says whether the host gets a second team.** Treat "8 places" as **unconfirmed** for 2027; it could be 4. [M]
8. **IOAI deadlines vs Singapore's NTU timeline.**
   - IOAI wants national selection done by **1 Apr 2027** and the team "ready" by **15 Apr 2027**.
   - AISG/NTU show the Selection Test on **8 May** and the camp **14–18 Jun 2027**.
   - Not reconciled publicly. The likely explanation is that the country registers by April and names contestants in the "2nd phase of registration" (2 Apr – 19 May 2027), but this is **unverified**. [L]
9. **Awardee lists on the NOAI microsite (S7) are mislabelled.**
   - In the 2026 tab, the "Silver" table actually lists the second Bronze slide, the "Bronze" table lists the actual Silver winners, and 17 Bronze winners are missing.
   - In the 2025 tab, the "Gold" table holds 5 of the 6 Silver winners, the "Silver" table duplicates the 2026 bronze list, and the Bronze/Merit tables contain duplicates.
   - **The official award slides on the AISG page (S4, uploaded Mar 2026) are authoritative.** Counts: 2026 G12/S24/B37/P73 = 146; 2025 G3/S6/B9/M17/P81 = 116.
10. **Number of schools in 2026: "33" vs 32 listed.**
    - The microsite list (S3) omits Raffles Girls' School (Secondary). The AISG list (S4) includes it, giving 33. **33 is correct.**
11. **2026 Final programming tasks: 3 vs "5".**

    - Official sources and the released notebooks show **3 tasks** with multiple parts. The blogger probably counted sub-parts. **Official: 3.** [H]

## 8. What a student needs to know right now (14 Oct 2026)

1. **Get registered. It is school-based.**
   - MOE students: ask your school's NOAI teacher representative now. The school interest window is open until **15 Jan 2027**.
   - As of 29 Sep 2026 only **12 schools** had signed up. If yours is not on the list, ask a teacher to email **noai@aisingapore.org**.
   - Individual sign-ups are **not** accepted from MOE students.
2. **Not in an MOE school?** (home-schooled SC/PR, between Sec 4 and JC/Poly, private or international school)
   - Email noai@aisingapore.org **by 27 Nov 2026**, which is 44 days away.
   - Approved students sit the Prelim at the AISG NUS Office.
3. **Prelim: Fri 19 Feb 2027, 9:30 am, about 18 weeks away.**
   - **150 MCQs in 90 minutes**, about **36 seconds per question**, on Google Forms in your school lab.
   - **No calculator**, no notes, no AI. Pen and paper only.
   - **No negative marking**, so answer every question. Ties may be broken on time taken.
   - This round rewards **fast recall across all domains**: maths (linear algebra, calculus, probability, optimisation), Python/NumPy/Pandas/PyTorch and debugging, classical ML/NN/CV/NLP/GenAI, and algorithms and system design.
   - Practise mental arithmetic and reading code quickly.
4. **Final: Fri 5 Mar 2027.**
   - Top 150 only. **20 MCQs + 3 Python programming tasks.** In 2026 this was 3 h, worth 100 marks, at NTU, with no internet.
   - Past tasks asked students to implement algorithms **from scratch** in NumPy/PyTorch: gradient boosting, mean IoU, U-Net debugging, scaled dot-product and multi-head attention, gradient-descent linear regression, K-means and RAG.
   - Practise in Jupyter **without AI autocomplete**.
5. **Use the official materials first.**
   - The 2026 Prelim (300 MCQs; interactive Self-Assessment Tool), the 2026 Final MCQs and notebooks, and the 2025 MCQs and notebooks are all public (links in §4).
   - Then use the IOAI 2026 Syllabus, the IOAI past tasks, and AICC monthly contests.
6. **After NOAI (results early Apr 2027)**, the top 60 go to NTU:
   - Selection Test on Sat 8 May 2027.
   - Saturday training on 22 and 29 May and 5 and 12 Jun.
   - Residential camp 14–18 Jun.
   - **IOAI 2027 is in Singapore, 4–10 Jul 2027.**
   - Only **Singapore Citizens and PRs** can be on the team. IOAI also requires you to be **20 or under on 1 Jul 2027**.
   - From 2027 IOAI allows **one team per country** (normally up to 4), so team places may be fewer than the usual 8 (unconfirmed).
   - Past results show the NTU stage re-ranks students: a NOAI Bronze winner placed 2nd in the world at IOAI 2025.
7. **What you receive.**
   - Finalists get only a tier (Gold, Silver, Bronze or Participation) and a certificate. No scores.
   - Prelim-only participants get **no certificate**.
   - Appeals are on technical grounds only, within 7 days.
8. **Watch for updates.**
   - The NOAI 2027 page says "*More details to be released nearer to date".
   - IOAI 2027 registration details come out 2 Nov 2026, and an IOAI 2027 syllabus may follow.
   - Check https://noai.aisingapore.org/noai-2027/ and https://noai.aisingapore.org/faqs/, **not** the older AISG page.
